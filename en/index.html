The shows you already watch,
one line at a time

ShadowCue is a Chrome extension for practising English with Netflix subtitles: replay each line, fill in missing words at your level, and speak along to see how accurate you were. No sign-up, and your progress stays on your computer.

Three ways to practise

Listen → fill in → speak. The same scene, practised three ways.

🎧 Listen

Replay what you missed

It pauses when a line ends. R plays it again, Enter goes on. Slow it down (0.9×–0.5×), hide the text, or repeat a line.

✏️ Fill-in quiz

Catch words at your level

From Starter (A1) to Advanced (C1+), words near your level become blanks — from one blank up to writing the whole line.

🎙 Speak

Say it, see your score

Repeat after the actor and see which words matched, sounded different or were missed, then play your own recording.

Everyday expressions

ShadowCue scans the whole episode for lines with phrases you can use in daily conversation. Click one to jump to the scene, star it to review later.

  • Tags like figure out, by the way, fair enough
  • Show others for a fresh set; saving one adds another

Speaking score

Chrome's speech recognition turns what you said into text and compares it with the line. Different words get a wavy underline; missed words are struck through.

  • Accuracy and what you said
  • Play back your own voice

How it works

  1. Add ShadowCue to Chrome.
  2. Open an English title on Netflix. The panel appears on the right.
  3. Choose a mode and your level.
  4. It pauses after each line. R to replay, Enter for the next.

Your daily goal (minutes or blanks) and streak are under the extension icon. The panel speaks English or Korean.

Your data stays on your computer

ShadowCue has no server, no account and no usage tracking.

Storage

Saved lines, quiz history and study time live in your browser only and are deleted with the extension.

Microphone

On only while you press 🎙 Speak. Recordings are never saved. Speech-to-text uses Chrome's speech recognition (Google).

Subtitles

Used only to practise the title you're watching, on screen. Never sent anywhere or saved as a whole.

FAQ

Is this made by Netflix?

No. ShadowCue is an independent learning tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Netflix. It shows a practice panel over the Netflix player.

Does it work on phones or tablets?

Not yet — Chrome on desktop only.

What about titles without English subtitles?

It needs English subtitles (or English CC) on the title.

Can I use it without a microphone?

Yes. Listening and the fill-in quiz don't need one.
